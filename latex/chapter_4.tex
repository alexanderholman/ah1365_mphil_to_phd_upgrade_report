\section{Further Steps Towards Rank Preservation} \label{section:rank_preservation}

\subsection{Motivation: Why Rank Preservation Is Central}

The ability to preserve DFT-calculated interface rankings is critical for efficient high-throughput screening of material systems. Absolute interface energies are less relevant in isolation if the relative favourability of competing configurations is misrepresented. This motivates the need for improving rank fidelity in machine-learned models such as MACE \cite{Batatia2022-xz, Maji2021-kc, Maji2021-vq, Cervantes2025-ze, Leitherer2023-ew, Batzner2022-fd, Lorentzon2025-si, Kao2025-ay, Nykiel2023-jf, Uhrin2025-ba, Gerber2023-yd, Owen2024-wp, Rosen2022-pw, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Hepplestone2010-vw, Schusteritsch2015-lw, Hu2021-oj, Kohn1965-ss, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Li2020-ls, Taylor2017-oi, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Zhang2016-lt, Dirac1930-re, Saidi2017-ab, Annaberdiyev2021-ne, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsubsection{General MACE model (baseline)}

This baseline model is trained on broad-spectrum, bulk-focused data. While it demonstrates some transferability, its limitations are apparent in systems with interfacial complexity; especially silicon-rich or structurally diverse cases \cite{Batatia2022-xz, Owen2024-wp, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Saidi2017-ab, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsubsection{Interface-specific fine-tuning}

Here, the same model is refined using additional interface-representative configurations, tailored to improve accuracy in the interfacial regime. This targets data-domain mismatch as a key source of ranking degradation \cite{Batatia2022-xz, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Saidi2017-ab, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsubsection{Delta-learning (ΔE = E\_DFT − E\_MLP; Pitfield 2024-inspired)}

This strategy applies a correction to existing MLP predictions by learning residuals between MLP and DFT energies. It allows the reuse of baseline MLPs while introducing physics-informed calibration, improving ranking accuracy without fully retraining the model \cite{Lorentzon2025-si, Batatia2022-xz, Uhrin2025-ba, Gerber2023-yd, Owen2024-wp, Rosen2022-pw, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Saidi2017-ab, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\section{Integration of RAFFLE into Structure Generation}

\subsection{Motivation for RAFFLE Use: Enhanced Interface Diversity}

ARTEMIS-generated stacks, though consistent, impose alignment constraints. RAFFLE offers a mechanism to interpolate interfacial zones, increase disorder, or induce registry shifts \cite{Lorentzon2025-si, Batatia2022-xz, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Hu2021-oj, Kohn1965-ss, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Zhang2016-lt, Saidi2017-ab, Annaberdiyev2021-ne, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsection{Current ARTEMIS-Only Pipeline: Limitations}

\section{Towards a Machine-Learned Interface (MLI) Predictor}

\subsection{Objective: Predict Likely Interface Configurations from Material Pairs}

The longer-term aim is to develop a model that, given a pair of slab surfaces, can predict the favourability of resulting interfaces without full relaxation or enumeration \cite{Lorentzon2025-si, Batatia2022-xz, Uhrin2025-ba, Gerber2023-yd, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Hu2021-oj, Kohn1965-ss, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Zhang2016-lt, Saidi2017-ab, Annaberdiyev2021-ne, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsection{Feature and Descriptor Design}

\subsubsection{Surface energies, Miller planes, atomic densities}

Descriptor sets will include known surface energetics and crystallographic identifiers, all extractable from existing data (\texttt{surface\_energy\_matrix.csv}) \cite{Batatia2022-xz, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Hu2021-oj, Kohn1965-ss, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Zhang2016-lt, Saidi2017-ab, Annaberdiyev2021-ne, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsubsection{Composition vectors, stacking vectors, registry, strain metrics}

Additional features will quantify interfacial registry, stoichiometry, mismatch, and coordination at the interface centre. \subsection{Model Development Roadmap}

\subsubsection{Classification vs regression}

Early models may predict favourable/unfavourable (binary), or regress $\Delta E_\mathrm{IF}$ directly. Shallow decision trees or SVMs will be trialled before advancing to graph-based models \cite{Batatia2022-xz, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Hu2021-oj, Kohn1965-ss, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Zhang2016-lt, Saidi2017-ab, Annaberdiyev2021-ne, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.

\subsubsection{Feasibility for rapid interface suggestion and ranking}

The MLI predictor is envisioned as a low-cost filter to prioritise candidates before full relaxation, possibly integrating with ARTEMIS + RAFFLE pipelines \cite{Lorentzon2025-si, Batatia2022-xz, Butler2019-ws, Di_Liberto2022-gz, Kresse1996-wm, Chagarov2015-ol, Pyzer-Knapp2025-uq, Davies2021-zh, Ceperley1980-bs, Behler2007-nu, Turlo2019-pd, Pitfield2025-ah, Alam2020-ci, Zhao2019-pp, Van_de_Walle_CG1986-ae, Kresse1993-nu, Blochl1994-kt, Kresse1999-zf, Hu2021-oj, Kohn1965-ss, Wang2013-gt, Perdew1981-iu, Slater1951-jc, Hartree1928-ae, Kuhbach2021-gz, Peschel2024-et, Zhang2016-lt, Saidi2017-ab, Annaberdiyev2021-ne, Zuo2019-wo, Schutt2018-xc, Zotti2018-nv, Wang2024-tn}.
